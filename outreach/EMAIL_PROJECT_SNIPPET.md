I built SignalRoom, an independent historical set-piece evidence workbench using attributed StatsBomb Open Data. It loads the complete WSL 2023/24 season, compares recorded corner-delivery groups with match-aware uncertainty, and links each displayed reference to a reviewable derived evidence summary. It is a portfolio prototype rather than a claim of current tactical insight or club readiness, and I would value feedback on whether the workflow helps analysts retrieve and communicate evidence more clearly.
