# Remaining risks and readiness decision

## Readiness

**Ready for:** public historical portfolio demonstration and controlled analyst usability testing.

**Not ready for:** current opponent preparation, operational club use, commercial use, or external-validation claims.

## Residual risks

1. Professional analysts may already complete the task faster in integrated provider tools.
2. The rights design is conservative technical risk control, not legal advice or written provider permission.
3. Historical event data cannot reveal continuous movement, screens, assignments, or intent.
4. Twelve WSL team-season units support descriptive percentiles, not defensible ML.
5. The generated manual corpus has not been double-reviewed.
6. Lineup interval minutes require further validation before being treated as official minutes played.
7. Current men's competition data and authorized synchronized video remain unavailable.
8. Streamlit analyst notes are session-only.

## Next gate

Run the independent analyst protocol. In parallel, seek written permission or an authorized event-plus-video package with explicit storage, display, export, retention, deletion, derived-output, and training terms.
