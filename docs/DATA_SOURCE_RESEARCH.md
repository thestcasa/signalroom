# Data-source and rights boundary

## Implemented source

StatsBomb Open Data is pinned and attributed. Events and lineups are ingested from the official repository. Public analysis is allowed with attribution, while underlying data may not be reproduced or redistributed. The public build therefore exposes aggregates and derived summaries only.

## Current coverage

| Case | Source coverage | Peer decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Brighton Women, WSL 2023/24 | Complete 132-fixture, 12-team season | Descriptive leave-target-out baseline allowed |
| Bayer Leverkusen, Bundesliga 2023/24 | 34 released Leverkusen matches | League peer baseline suppressed |

## Researched but not implemented

- Wyscout research data: potential second event adapter under its separate public research licence.
- SkillCorner and Metrica samples: useful for separate tracking and synchronization research.
- SoccerNet: suitable only for separately scoped research tasks with applicable access terms.
- Commercial provider and club packages: require exact contractual rights, schema, coverage, quality, retention, public-display, derived-output, and model-training terms.

Different providers must not be combined into one tactical population unless they represent the same authorized population under validated definitions.
