# Analyst validation protocol

1. Recruit two or three opposition or set-piece analysts who were not involved in development.
2. Provide the same historical task, cutoff, and time limit.
3. Randomize whether SignalRoom or the baseline workflow is used first.
4. Record task time, observations, evidence opened, unsupported claims, and export quality.
5. Ask each reviewer to classify the population, uncertainty, evidence limitations, and missing modality.
6. Preserve disagreements and qualitative feedback without averaging them away.
7. Publish only anonymized aggregate results with consent.

The validation outcome is `pending`. A polished interface, successful test suite, or friendly reaction is not analyst validation.
